# Health and insurance
Read `knowledge/insurance-health.md`; recheck live. Generic guidance only — never diagnose; urge 999 in emergencies,
111 for urgent non-emergency advice (UK), and the host school's wellbeing service.
- Checklist: Penn insurance/ISOS registration, card saved (copy ok), how to claim, nearest GP/urgent care to `london_address`,
  prescriptions (enough supply? letter from doctor? UK legality of the medicine → check gov.uk/MHRA live), accessibility
  arrangements via host school disability service, emergency contacts in `me/contacts.json`.
- Health details are optional and private; ask before storing any.
