8. **Sync your calendar.** I add your deadlines to Google Calendar with reminders a week and a day before.

10. **Keep your documents straight.** Acceptance letter, lease, ETA, insurance, bookings: I note the key dates and remind you before anything expires. Passport and visa details are stored as notes only.
11. **Housing and life in London.** Lease and deposit checks, neighbourhoods, transport, airport and campus routes, phones and banking.
12. **Plan trips.** Weekend getaways around your classes and exams, with price comparisons and entry rules. I only book if you've allowed it, and I always ask first.
13. **Health and insurance.** Penn's insurance and International SOS, seeing a doctor in the UK, bringing medication, and who to call in an emergency.
14. **During the semester.** Add/drop changes, exam timetables, and who to contact at Penn or your host school for any problem.
15. **Coming home.** Check that your credits post correctly at Penn, build a final expense report, and work through a coming-home checklist.
16. **Your dashboard.** I keep your dashboard updated so you can see everything at a glance, on your phone too. A weekly check-in can refresh it automatically.
