## Module registration rules [FACT]
- 60 credits = 4 × 15-credit modules; no back-ups; first-come, first-served, approved by each academic School (allow up to 10 working days).
- Can request changes throughout the window, but only after earlier requests are approved/rejected. After close → only Add/Drop.
- After Add/Drop no drops; final exams must be taken. Over/under-load needs written home-school permission (overload costs extra).
- Prerequisites: upload a syllabus as evidence. Registration doesn't check timetable clashes.
- Levels: 4 = first year, 5 = second, 6 = third, 7 = postgraduate (not open to UG associates).
- Syllabi: study-abroad@qmul.ac.uk (≤8 per email, code + name) or AskQM to the academic department; UK outlines are less detailed.
