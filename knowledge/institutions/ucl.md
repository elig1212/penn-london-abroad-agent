# University College London (UCL) — affiliate (study abroad) students
- [FACT] Affiliates study **Term 1 only, Terms 2 and 3, or the full year**; **Term 2 only is not possible**. A "spring" student is
  therefore usually Terms 2 & 3 (January–June). (ucl.ac.uk/prospective-students/study-abroad-at-ucl/glossary, checked 2026-11-10)
  [UNVERIFIED] whether Penn's UCL exchange arrangement differs — check the student's acceptance letter/Penn Abroad portal.
- [FACT] UG affiliates taking Terms 2 & 3 select **30–60 credits**. (UCL Academic Manual ch.3 s.4 Affiliate Student Registration)
- [FACT] Module selection windows: Sept/Oct and Dec/Jan; learning agreements signed after modules are approved. (UCL fact sheet 2026/27)
- [UNVERIFIED] 2026/27 Term 2 start and exam dates — 2025/26 Terms 2&3 ran 12 Jan–12 Jun 2026 with exams/assessments in Term 3.
  Confirm on ucl.ac.uk term dates.
- Module catalogue: ucl.ac.uk/module-catalogue (module pages show term, credits, assessment). Tool: `python3 scripts/host_module.py ucl CODE`.
- XCAT precedents: data-shared/xcat-precedents-ucl.csv (22 records; no ECON-credit precedents; 2026-11-10).
- Affiliates support: ucl.ac.uk/students/international/isop/information-affiliates.
