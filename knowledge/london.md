# London practicalities
## Transport
- [FACT] **18+ Student Oyster photocard**: 30% off TfL adult-rate Travelcards and bus & tram passes; requires being 18+, living in a
  London borough in term time, and studying at a TfL-registered institution (£25 non-refundable fee). (tfl.gov.uk/fares/free-and-discounted-travel/18-plus-student-oyster-photocard, checked 2026-11-10)
  [UNVERIFIED] whether semester/associate students at each host school qualify — check the host school's discounted-travel page
  (QMUL: qmul.ac.uk/registry-services/student-information/my-finances/discounted-travel).
- Contactless bank cards and phones work on all TfL services with daily/weekly capping. [UNVERIFIED] current cap amounts — check tfl.gov.uk/fares.
- Airports: use TfL Journey Planner / airport websites for live routes and fares (Heathrow: Elizabeth line or Piccadilly line;
  Gatwick/Stansted/Luton: rail links). Always give door-to-door time and cost.
## Housing law (England) [FACT] (gov.uk/tenancy-deposit-protection, checked 2026-11-10)
- Landlords must protect a tenancy deposit in an approved scheme — **Deposit Protection Service, mydeposits, or Tenancy Deposit
  Scheme** — within **30 days** of receiving it, and give the tenant the "prescribed information".
- At the end, the deposit must be returned within **10 days** of agreeing the amount; disputes go to the scheme's free dispute service.
- If a landlord fails to protect it, a court can order repayment plus a penalty of up to 3× the deposit.
- [FACT] gov.uk notes assured shorthold tenancies were replaced on 1 May 2026 (Renters' Rights reforms) — older guides may be out of date; check gov.uk for current tenancy types and notice rules.
- Scams: never pay a deposit before viewing (in person or a trusted proxy) and verifying the landlord/agent.
## Phones and money [UNVERIFIED — check live]
- Unlocked phone + UK SIM/eSIM (many monthly no-contract plans) is usually cheapest; check carrier international plans before leaving.
- Use a card with no foreign-transaction fees; withdraw cash from bank ATMs, not exchange kiosks. UK bank accounts often need proof of a UK address and immigration status.
## Safety
- Emergency 999; police non-emergency 101. Register trips in Penn's MyTrips (also needed for ISOS insurance).
