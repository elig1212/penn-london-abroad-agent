# Consumer Psychology minor (College + Wharton interschool)
Source: catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/consumer-psychology-minor (2026-11-10)
- [FACT] 9 CU: stats course; PSYC 0001; PSYC 1440 or 2400; one of PSYC 1310/1530/1333/2737/2740/2750/2555; +1 APCP elective;
  MKTG 1010, MKTG 2110, one data/methods MKTG (e.g. 2120), one APCN marketing elective.
- [FACT] College students: 2 of the 4 PSYC courses must count only toward this minor.
- [PRECEDENT] QMUL LIN4215 Psychology of Language → PSYC 1310; Goldsmiths PS52003T Social Psychology → PSYC 1440;
  Goldsmiths IM53004B Psychology of Marketing and Advertising → PSYC 2984 (see data-shared/).
