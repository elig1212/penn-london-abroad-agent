# Penn Economics major (BA) — rules relevant to study abroad
Sources: catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/economics-ba ; economics.sas.upenn.edu/undergraduate/course-information/credit-external-courses (both 2026-11-10)
- [FACT] Major: ECON 0100/0200 (or waiver), ECON 2100, 2200, 2300 (or STAT 4300+4310), 2310, four 4000-level ECON electives, MATH calculus ×2.
- [FACT] At least 6 ECON course units must be taken at Penn.
- [FACT] Study-abroad credit as ECON 4982 (Study Abroad Major Credit) counts as a major elective; ECON 0982 does **not** (counts only toward ECON/EPOL minors).
- [FACT] No credit for: courses without 2 CU intro econ as prerequisite; **business courses (e.g. banking — get Wharton credit instead)**; purely descriptive or geography courses; online courses.
- [FACT] Approval must be obtained before departing; submit detailed syllabus.
