# Entering the UK (semester students)
- [FACT] QMUL (Countdown #1, 19 Oct 2026): most incoming associate students use an **Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)**;
  no paid or unpaid work allowed with an ETA or Visitor visa. Semester students who want to work need a Student visa and must
  request a CAS from QMUL before 1 Dec. Must have permission granted before travelling; QMUL can't register students without it.
  Use gov.uk/check-uk-visa to see what applies. Dual nationals with British citizenship must enter on the British passport.
- [UNVERIFIED] ETA fee, validity period and processing time — check gov.uk/eta live before applying (they have changed).
- Store ETA as a note-only document: approval date, expiry, which passport it's linked to (not numbers).
