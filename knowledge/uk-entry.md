# Entering the UK (semester students)
- [FACT] Most incoming associate/semester students use an **Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)**; no paid or unpaid work is
  allowed on an ETA or Visitor visa. Semester students who want to work need a Student visa (QMUL: request a CAS before 1 Dec).
  Permission must be granted before travelling. Use gov.uk/check-uk-visa. (QMUL Countdown #1, 19 Oct 2026)
- [FACT] ETA fee **£20** from 8 Apr 2026 (was £16). Source: gov.uk "Home Office immigration and nationality fees, 8 April 2026".
  Some gov.uk pages still show £16 — the fee table is authoritative.
- [FACT] Decision usually within 1 day, up to 3 working days; often minutes via the UK ETA app. Apply only via gov.uk/eta or
  the official UK ETA app (other sites charge more). No refunds. (gov.uk/eta/apply, checked 2026-11-10)
- [FACT] Valid 2 years or until the passport expires, multiple entries; linked to the passport used to apply — travel on that
  passport. An ETA doesn't guarantee entry. (gov.uk/eta)
- Dual nationals with British citizenship must enter on the British passport (QMUL Countdown #1).
- Store ETA as a note-only document: approval date, expiry, which passport (not numbers). Check status: gov.uk/check-eta.
