# XCAT (External Course Approval Tool)
- [FACT] Penn credit for courses taken elsewhere is requested through XCAT; study-abroad credit must be approved **before**
  taking the course / departing. Penn Econ evaluation "generally takes 2 weeks". Source: economics.sas.upenn.edu/undergraduate/course-information/credit-external-courses (2026-11-10)
- [FACT] Public precedent search (no login): xcat.at.upenn.edu/searchPrecedents — filters: credit type, country, institution,
  external course id/title, Penn course. Shows expiration dates. (2026-11-10)
- [FACT] Path@Penn → Academic Planning & Registration → XCAT opens the logged-in tool. (2026-11-10)
- Practical: advisors can't confirm approval in advance — the only way to know is to submit and wait (student experience, 2026-11).
- Submit detailed syllabus: prerequisites, contact hours, topics, assignments, exams, grading (Econ dept guidance).
- After the course: credit posts only once the official host transcript reaches Penn; follow up with College / SRFS registrar if delayed.
